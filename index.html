Data sources & method

Real data only. Every number on this page is pulled programmatically from the sources below at generation time — nothing is mocked or hand-entered.
Method notes. NDQ/HNDQ returns are total returns computed from Yahoo Finance adjusted closes (distributions reinvested). The 200-week MA is a simple moving average of Friday-week closes; MA history begins March 2019 once 200 weeks of NDQ data exist. AustralianSuper option returns are daily crediting rates from AustralianSuper's own performance API — these are net of investment fees, costs and tax, so they are not a perfectly like-for-like comparison with gross ETF returns. AustralianSuper options are only available inside super. This page is information, not financial advice. Past performance isn't a reliable indicator of future performance.
